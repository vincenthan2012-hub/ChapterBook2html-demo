FACT
Where does Harry sleep in the Dursley household?
哈利在德思礼家住在哪里？
Answer
He sleeps in the cupboard under the stairs.
他睡在楼梯下的橱柜里。
INFER
Why does Harry feel a 'leap' in his heart when he hears Mrs. Figg cannot take him?
当哈利听说费格太太不能照顾他时，为什么他感到心中‘一跳’？
Answer
Because he hates visiting Mrs. Figg's house, which smells of cabbage and is filled with photos of cats.
因为他讨厌去费格太太家，那里闻起来像卷心菜，而且到处是猫的照片。
TONE
How do the Dursleys view Harry based on the way they speak about him?
根据德思礼一家谈论哈利的方式，他们是如何看待他的？
Answer
They view him as something inferior and unpleasant, comparing him to a 'slug' and speaking as if he isn't there.
他们把他看作低等且令人不快的东西，把他比作‘蛞蝓’，说话时就像他不存在一样。
REFLECT
Harry's hair grows back instantly after being cut. How does this contrast with the Dursleys' reaction to it?
哈利被剪发后头发立即长回来了。这与德思礼一家的反应形成了怎样的对比？
Answer
While the event is magical and mysterious, the Dursleys treat it as a behavioral problem and punish him by locking him in the cupboard.
虽然这件事具有魔力且神秘，但德思礼一家将其视为行为问题，并处罚他将其关在橱柜里。
